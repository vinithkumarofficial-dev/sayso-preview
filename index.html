Questions

Straight answers.

Who actually does the work?

Your project is led from Dubai, where strategy, project management and client success sit. Design and engineering happen across our teams in Chennai, Coimbatore, Singapore and Malaysia, and our own film team makes the film and photography. You have one point of contact here and a team of forty behind them.

Is Sayso Creative a new company?

The Dubai studio is new. The team is not. Sayso Creative is a brand of Najaha Solutions CO. L.L.C., operating in Dubai since 2020, and the delivery team has been shipping websites, stores, apps and brands for years. The founder has worked hands on in WordPress, WooCommerce and Shopify since 2016.

How long does a website take?

Most brochure websites take four to six weeks from sign off to launch. Online stores usually take six to ten weeks depending on the catalogue and integrations. Apps and portals are scoped individually and you get a fixed timeline before design starts.

Which platforms do you build on?

WordPress and WooCommerce for most websites and stores, Shopify where the brand wants a hosted store, and custom builds where the product needs them. For apps we build native iOS and Android and cross platform. We recommend the platform that fits your team, not the one we like most.

Do you work with businesses outside the UAE?

Yes. Our work spans the UAE, India and beyond, with clients as far as the Philippines. Meetings happen on your time zone and everything is delivered online.

Can you take over a site someone else built?

Yes. We start with an audit of speed, security and search, then agree what to fix first. Many of our maintenance clients came to us this way.

Will my site be fast?

We build to a published budget: Lighthouse mobile 95 or higher, Largest Contentful Paint under 1.8 seconds, under 1 MB on the home page. We test on a mid range Android phone on 4G, not only on a fast laptop.

How do we start?

Send a short brief or book a free 30 minute call. We come back with questions, then a plan with scope, price and timeline. No charge until you sign it off.
